Where the pages actually agree

Every other page here computes one technique in isolation. This one runs eleven independent signal types, every one of them already computed somewhere on this dashboard, against every planet, and flags anything three or more of them agree on. It's not three hand-picked stories anymore: it's a live tally, recomputed for whatever date you pick, that shows its work rather than asserting significance.

How the tally works

Eleven signal types, each already computed on its own page. A planet gets credit for a signal only when it clears a real, stated bar, not just "has a value." Three or more independent signal types agreeing is the threshold for a flag, a deliberately conservative bar, chosen so a flag means several genuinely different techniques converged, not just one loud one.
