Aspect patterns

Named configurations of three or more aspects at once, not just individual lines. A Grand Trine is three points in a closed triangle of trines. A T-square is two points in opposition, both squared by a third that absorbs the pressure. A Grand Cross is two full oppositions, each squared to the other. A Yod ("Finger of Fate") is two points in easy sextile, both quincunx to a third apex. Computed from whichever points are currently loaded above, most charts don't have one of these at all, that's expected, not a bug.

Planetary returns

A return happens when a transiting planet arrives back at the exact degree it held when you were born. Most dates below use each planet's average orbital period, which can miss by months to a year, planets move unevenly (their orbits aren't perfectly circular) and often station retrograde right around a return, turning one date into a three-part approach/retreat/return sequence. The Saturn Return card below has been checked against real 2034–2035 ephemeris data rather than the averaged formula, the rest are still estimates.
