Fortune, Spirit & the five lots derived from them

The Hermetic tradition (Paulus, Olympiodorus) builds seven core lots from your Ascendant and five planets. Two are primary, Fortune and Spirit, and five more (Eros, Victory, Necessity, Courage, Nemesis) are each built by taking a planet's distance from Fortune or Spirit and projecting it from your Ascendant.
