The number carried since birth

Life Path is numerology's closest equivalent to a Sun sign, the core number derived purely from a birth date, no name required. It has two real, competing calculation conventions that can produce a genuinely different number for the same person, shown here side by side rather than silently picked for you, the same way the Dispositor Tree shows modern and traditional rulership as two real answers instead of one.

Where the methods agree: Pinnacle age ranges

Pinnacles are four life phases built from the birth date, not yet fully built out on this page (see below), but their age boundaries are worth previewing here because of a real technical nuance: the arithmetic always uses the Life Path reduced to a plain single digit, even when the headline number itself is a preserved master number. That means the age brackets come out identical under Method A and Method B for this birth date, only the headline number and its archetype differ, not the life-stage timeline underneath it.
